# Code Modification Instructions

## 1. Reorder Instruction Content

1.1 On the instruction page, ensure that the explanation of what happens
in a round appears **before** listing the decisions participants must
make.

1.2 After explaining the round flow, list the decisions clearly and
separately.

1.3 Ensure the decision section is explicit and free of ambiguity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Clarify Deposit and Expenditure Decisions

2.1 In the instruction page, clearly state:

-   Participants choose how much to deposit into the account.
-   The remaining amount becomes cash in hand.

2.2 Explicitly clarify:

-   The expenditure decision is how much to spend from cash in hand
    only.
-   Spending cannot exceed cash in hand.

2.3 Add clear instruction:

-   If a participant spends any positive amount, they must type the word
    exactly:

    `SPEND`

-   The word must match exactly as required (case-sensitive if
    applicable).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Remove Duplicate "ECU" (Decision Pages)

3.1 On the decision pages (not the instruction page), "ECU" appears
twice unnecessarily.

3.2 Remove the duplicate occurrence so that "ECU" appears only where
required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Add Conversion Rate

4.1 On the instruction page, explicitly mention the specific conversion
rate from ECU to real currency.

4.2 The rate must be clearly displayed and unambiguous.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Baseline and Sudden Shock Conditions

5.1 In both Baseline and Sudden Shock treatments, clarify in the
instruction page:

-   There may or may not be a liquidity shock during the experiment.
-   The shock may occur at some point in between.
-   It is not guaranteed.

5.2 Do not structure anything as pre-shock or post-shock rounds in the
instructions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Pre-Announced Treatment Modifications

6.1 In the instruction page, clearly state:

-   After Round 7, the old lab currency becomes invalid.

6.2 Clarify:

-   After Round 7, the old currency cannot be used for spending.
-   It cannot be used further unless deposited.

6.3 Add:

-   The only way to use the old currency after Round 7 is to deposit it
    into the account.

6.4 Clearly state:

-   Depositing old currency after Round 7 subjects the participant to a
    higher audit probability for the next three rounds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Round Header Changes (All Treatments)

7.1 At the top of each round screen:

-   Do not display labels such as "Pre-Shock Round 1" or "Post-Shock
    Round 8".

7.2 Instead, display only:

-   "Round 1"
-   "Round 2"
-   ...
-   "Round n"

7.3 Apply this change to all rounds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Shock Announcement Screen Change

8.1 After Round 7, on the shock announcement screen:

-   Change the heading from:

    "Demonetisation Shock"

-   To:

    "Liquidity Shock"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Comprehension Check Logic Change

9.1 Modify the comprehension check so that participants can enter their
answer freely.

9.2 After submission:

-   If correct → display confirmation that the answer is correct.
-   If incorrect → display that it is wrong and show the correct answer.

9.3 Feedback must be immediate and automatic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Clarity Requirement

10.1 Ensure all instruction text is:

-   Direct
-   Explicit
-   Clearly sequenced
-   Free of ambiguity.

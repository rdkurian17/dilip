Instructions

In this task, you will participate in 15 rounds. In each round, you receive income in a lab currency called ECU (Experimental Currency Unit).

At the end, your total wealth will be converted into British pounds (£) at the exchange rate: every 100 ECU = 75p

Each Round:

1. Income: You receive 100 ECU in cash.

2. Deposit Decision: Decide how much to deposit.

  • Deposited amount is taxed at 30%
  • Remaining stays as cash in hand and is not declared for tax purposes
  • Deposits are safe from audit
  • Cash has audit risk

3. Mandatory Spending: Spend exactly 40 ECU.

  • Choose how much from deposit
  • Choose how much from cash
  • Must sum to 40 ECU
  • Cash payments require typing a verification code

4. Random Audit: 5% chance each round.

  • Pay 30% tax on all cash in hand
  • Pay fine = 2× the unpaid tax
  • Total penalty deducted from deposit first
  • After penalty is paid, remaining cash is automatically moved into your deposit account (tax-free)

5. Round Summary: See your updated balances.

Example:
Start: Deposit = 70, Cash = 30
Deposit 60 → Tax 18 → Add 42 → New: Deposit = 112, Cash = 70
Spend: 20 cash + 20 deposit → New: Deposit = 92, Cash = 50
If audited: Tax = 15, Fine = 30, Penalty = 45
Deposit after penalty = 92 − 45 = 47
Remaining cash (50) moved to deposit → Final: Deposit = 97, Cash = 0

Audit Rules for Rounds 8–10

For the next three rounds, an audit applies to both:

  1. your cash in hand, and
  2. any converted amount that has not yet been taxed.

If audited in Rounds 8–10:

  • Tax = 30% of the sum (cash in hand + untaxed converted amount)
  • Fine = 2 times the tax
  • So, the total penalty equals 90% of (cash in hand + untaxed converted amount)
  • The penalty is deducted from your deposit account first. If needed, the remainder is deducted from your cash in hand.

After the penalty is paid:

  • All remaining cash in hand is automatically transferred into your deposit account (tax-free, since the penalty has already been served).
  • The converted amount, which is now taxed, becomes fully regularised and cannot be taxed again in later rounds.

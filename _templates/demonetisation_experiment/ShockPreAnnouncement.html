Important: Upcoming Policy Change

After Round 7, the current lab currency (old ECU notes, which is held as cash in hand) will become invalid. Old currency (cash in hand) can no longer be used for spending.

The only way to preserve the value of old currency is to deposit it into your bank account. However, depositing old currency comes with two consequences for Rounds 8–10:

  • Higher audit probability: The more old currency you deposit, the higher your personal audit probability during Rounds 8–10.
  • Converted amount at risk: During Rounds 8–10, if you are audited, tax and fines are calculated on both your cash in hand and the amount of old currency you converted. The converted amount is only at risk once — if audited, it is considered fully regularised and cannot be charged again in a later round.

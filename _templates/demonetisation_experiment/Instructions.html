Welcome to the Experiment

Overview

You will make decisions over {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} rounds. Each round has these steps:

  1. Allocation: Decide how to save your income
  2. Spending: Make mandatory expenditure
  3. Audit: Random audit check
  4. Summary: See your new balances
Your Two Accounts
Bank Deposit
  • Taxed at 30%
  • Safe and formal
  • Easy to spend from
Cash in Hand
  • Not taxed initially
  • Faces 5% audit risk
  • Inconvenient to spend
How Each Round Works
Step 1: Allocation Decision

You receive {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} ECU in cash. Decide:

  • Deposit: Gets taxed 30%, but safe
  • Keep as cash: No tax now, but audit risk
Step 2: Mandatory Expenditure

You must spend exactly {{ C.MANDATORY_SPENDING }} ECU. Choose:

  • Cash: Requires verification code (inconvenient)
  • Deposit: Easy, automatic
Step 3: Audit

Random audit with 5% probability. If audited and you have cash:

  • Pay tax on ALL cash holdings
  • Pay fine = 2x the unpaid tax
  • Deducted from deposit
Complete Example
Worked Example

Starting: Deposit = 70 ECU, Cash = 30 ECU

1. Receive 100 ECU, deposit 60:

  • Tax: 60 x 30% = 18 ECU
  • Added: 60 - 18 = 42 ECU
  • Keep: 40 ECU cash
  • Result: Deposit = 112, Cash = 70

2. Spend 40 ECU (20 cash + 20 deposit):

  • Result: Deposit = 92, Cash = 50

3. Audited (unlucky!):

  • Tax on 50 cash: 15 ECU
  • Fine: 15 x 2 = 30 ECU
  • Total: 45 ECU from deposit
  • Final: Deposit = 47, Cash = 50

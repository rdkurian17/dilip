Welcome to the Experiment

You will participate in {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} rounds. In each round, you receive income in a lab currency called ECU (Experimental Currency Unit). Your objective is to manage your finances across rounds.

At the end of the experiment, your total wealth (Deposit + Cash) will be converted into British Pounds at the rate:

1 ECU = £0.01
Structure of Each Round

Each round proceeds as follows:

1. Income

You receive {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} ECU as cash.

2. Deposit Decision

You decide how much of the {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} ECU to deposit into your bank account.

  • The deposited amount is taxed at 30%.
  • Any amount not deposited remains as cash in hand.
  • Deposited funds are not subject to audit.
  • Cash holdings are subject to audit risk.
3. Mandatory Spending

In every round, you must spend exactly {{ C.MANDATORY_SPENDING }} ECU.

You must decide:

  • How much to pay from your deposit account.
  • How much to pay from cash in hand.

The two amounts must sum exactly to {{ C.MANDATORY_SPENDING }} ECU.

Important: If you choose to pay any positive amount from cash, you will be required to type a randomly generated sequence of letters exactly as shown (case-sensitive). This additional typing requirement simulates the inconvenience of handling physical cash compared to digital or formal payments.

4. Random Audit

Each round, there is a {{ C.BASE_AUDIT_PROB }}% chance of an audit.

If audited:

  • You must pay 30% tax on all cash currently held.
  • You must also pay a fine equal to 2 times the unpaid tax.
  • The total penalty is deducted from your deposit account first.
  • If the deposit balance is insufficient, the remaining penalty is deducted from cash.
5. Round Summary

Your updated deposit and cash balances are displayed before the next round begins.

Example
Example Round

Starting balances: Deposit = 70 ECU, Cash = 30 ECU

You deposit 60 ECU:

  • Tax = 18 ECU
  • 42 ECU added to deposit
  • 40 ECU remains as cash
  • New balances: Deposit = 112, Cash = 70

You choose to spend:

  • 20 ECU from cash
  • 20 ECU from deposit
  • New balances: Deposit = 92, Cash = 50

If audited:

  • Tax on 50 cash = 15 ECU
  • Fine = 30 ECU
  • Total penalty = 45 ECU
  • Penalty deducted from deposit first.

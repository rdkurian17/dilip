Welcome to the Experiment

Overview

In this experiment, you will participate in {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} rounds. Each round, you receive income in a lab currency called ECU (Experimental Currency Units). Your goal is to manage your finances across rounds. At the end, your total wealth is converted to real money.

How Each Round Works

Each round proceeds in the following order:

  1. Receive Income: You receive {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} as cash income.
  2. Deposit Decision: You choose how much of your income to deposit into your bank account. The deposited amount is taxed at 30%. Whatever you do not deposit remains as cash in hand.
  3. Mandatory Spending: You must spend exactly {{ C.MANDATORY_SPENDING }}. You choose how much of this to pay from your cash in hand. The remainder is automatically taken from your deposit account.
  4. Random Audit: There is a 5% chance of a random audit. If you are audited, you pay tax on all your cash holdings (30%) plus a fine equal to 2 times the unpaid tax. The penalty is deducted from your deposit account.
  5. Round Summary: You see your updated balances. Then the next round begins.
Your Two Accounts

You have two places to hold your ECU:

Bank Deposit
  • Taxed at 30% when deposited
  • Protected from audits
  • No additional cost when spending
Cash in Hand
  • Not taxed when received
  • 5% chance of audit each round
  • Requires typing SPEND to confirm payment
Your Decisions
Decision 1: Deposit Decision

Each round, you receive {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} in cash. You choose how much of this income to deposit into your bank account. The deposited amount is taxed at 30%. Whatever you do not deposit remains as cash in hand.

Decision 2: Expenditure Decision

Each round, you must spend exactly {{ C.MANDATORY_SPENDING }}. You choose how much of this mandatory spending to pay from your cash in hand. Your spending from cash cannot exceed your current cash balance. Any remainder of the {{ C.MANDATORY_SPENDING }} is automatically deducted from your deposit account.

If you choose to spend any positive amount from cash, you must type the word SPEND exactly (case-sensitive) to confirm.

Worked Example
Example Round

Starting balances: Deposit = 70 ECU, Cash = 30 ECU

1. Receive 100 ECU, deposit 60:

  • Tax on deposit: 60 x 30% = 18 ECU
  • Added to deposit: 60 - 18 = 42 ECU
  • Kept as cash: 40 ECU
  • Result: Deposit = 112, Cash = 70

2. Mandatory spending of 40 ECU (choose to spend 20 from cash):

  • 20 ECU paid from cash (must type SPEND to confirm)
  • Remaining 20 ECU automatically taken from deposit
  • Result: Deposit = 92, Cash = 50

3. Audit check (audited!):

  • Tax on 50 ECU cash: 50 x 30% = 15 ECU
  • Fine: 15 x 2 = 30 ECU
  • Total penalty: 45 ECU deducted from deposit
  • Final: Deposit = 47, Cash = 50

Participant Information Sheet

Study Title:

Liquidity under Constraint: Experimental Evidence on Behavioural Adaptation to Sudden Monetary Shocks

Principal Investigator: Richie Dilip Kurian, PhD Scholar, Department of Economics, University of Essex
Supervisor: Dr. Dennie Van Dolder, Department of Economics, University of Essex

1. Invitation to Participate

You are being invited to take part in a research study conducted by the Department of Economics at the University of Essex. Before deciding whether to participate, it is important that you understand why the research is being conducted and what it will involve. Please read the following information carefully and ask the researcher if anything is unclear or if you would like more information.

2. What Is the Purpose of the Study?

This study examines how individuals make financial decisions when faced with changes in monetary conditions and enforcement rules. Specifically, it investigates how people allocate income between different types of accounts and how they adjust their decisions when the environment changes unexpectedly.

The study aims to better understand how individuals respond to economic policy changes that affect liquidity and compliance decisions. The findings will contribute to academic research in economics and may inform policy discussions about financial regulation and formalisation of economic activity.

3. Why Have I Been Invited?

You have been invited because you are registered with the ESSEXLab participant pool. Participants must be at least 18 years old to take part in this study.

4. Do I Have to Take Part?

No. Participation in this study is entirely voluntary.

If you decide to take part, you will be asked to provide informed consent before the session begins. You are free to withdraw at any time during the experiment without giving a reason and without penalty.

If you withdraw before the end of the study, you will still receive the show-up payment. However, data already collected up to the point of withdrawal may still be used in anonymised form unless you explicitly request that it be deleted.

5. What Will Happen If I Take Part?

If you agree to participate, you will attend a laboratory session at ESSEXLab lasting approximately 30 minutes.

During the session:

  • You will receive instructions explaining the rules of the decision-making task.
  • You will complete comprehension questions to ensure understanding.
  • You will participate in 15 decision-making rounds involving allocation of experimental currency units between different accounts.
  • Your earnings will depend partly on your decisions and partly on random events within the experiment.
  • At the end of the session, you may complete a short questionnaire.
  • You will be paid privately in cash or via approved ESSEXLab payment procedures.

All interactions are conducted anonymously through computer terminals. You will not be required to interact directly with other participants.

6. What Are the Possible Risks or Disadvantages?

This study involves standard decision-making tasks commonly used in economic research.

  • There are no known physical risks.
  • Some participants may experience mild cognitive effort due to making financial decisions. You are free to take a short pause if needed.
  • There is no deception in this study. All rules and probabilities are explained clearly before decisions are made.
7. What Are the Possible Benefits?

There are no direct personal benefits beyond monetary compensation. However, your participation will contribute to academic research that may improve understanding of economic decision-making and policy design.

8. Payment

You will receive:

  • A fixed show-up fee.
  • Additional earnings based on your decisions in the experiment.

Average total earnings are expected to be approximately £10 for a 30-minute session. Exact earnings will depend on your choices and random outcomes within the experiment.

9. Confidentiality and Data Protection

All information collected in this study will be kept strictly confidential.

  • You will be identified only by a participant ID number.
  • Your name will not be linked to your responses.
  • Data will be anonymised before analysis.
  • Data will be stored securely on password-protected University of Essex systems.
  • Only the research team will have access to identifiable information.
  • Your data will be processed in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.
  • Anonymised data may be used in academic publications or shared in data repositories, but it will not be possible to identify you.
10. What Will Happen to the Results of the Study?

The results of this study may be used in:

  • A PhD thesis,
  • Academic journal publications,
  • Conference presentations.

No individual participant will be identifiable in any publication or report.

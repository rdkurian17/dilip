Instructions

You will participate in 15 rounds. In each round, you receive income in a lab currency called ECU (Experimental Currency Unit).

At the end, your total wealth will be converted at: 1 ECU = £0.01

Each Round:

1. Income: You receive 100 ECU as cash.

2. Deposit Decision: Decide how much to deposit.

  • Deposited amount is taxed at 30%
  • Remaining stays as cash
  • Deposits are safe from audit
  • Cash has audit risk

3. Mandatory Spending: Spend exactly 40 ECU.

  • Choose how much from deposit
  • Choose how much from cash
  • Must sum to 40 ECU
  • Cash payments require typing a verification code

4. Random Audit: 5% chance each round.

  • Pay 30% tax on all cash
  • Pay fine = 2× the unpaid tax
  • Total penalty deducted from deposit first

5. Round Summary: See your updated balances.

Example:
Start: Deposit = 70, Cash = 30
Deposit 60 → Tax 18 → Add 42
New: Deposit = 112, Cash = 70
Spend: 20 cash + 20 deposit
Final: Deposit = 92, Cash = 50
